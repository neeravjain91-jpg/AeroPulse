# Dataset Provenance and Roles

- **ACES Aircraft & Mechanical Data — NASA/NASA GHRC:** Altus II UAV campaign data. Primary UAV operational/engine-health development source. Dataset research supplied identifier `10.5067/ACES/MULTIPLE/DATA101`.
- **Marine Engine Fault — Zenodo / Aalto-associated research:** controlled marine-engine test-bench fault scenarios. Supporting fault-signature research only; not MALE-UAV diagnosis.
- **CWRU Bearing Data Center:** seeded bearing-fault vibration experiments. Supporting vibration/bearing methodology only; not target-engine validation.

Policy: the three datasets are not concatenated row-by-row. Their domain-specific outputs are kept separate and may be fused only at a higher decision layer.
